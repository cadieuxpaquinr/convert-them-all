          that build themselves as you read them will come out incomplete, and
          scripts are removed on purpose.

          wider the reach, the longer it takes and the more there is to go
          wrong.

        Save the whole section?
        
      
        THIS CAN TAKE A LONG TIME
        
      
        
      
        Every page under that address is fetched one at a time
        from someone else's server. Hundreds of pages means hundreds of
        requests, and several minutes.
        
      
        Saving only the page you need is recommended.
        It is instant, and it is what most people want.
        
      
        
        
        
      
        
            

    
      
        Permission removed successfully
        
      
        Conversions no longer have access to every site.
        
      
        
        
        BUT
        
        
        
        
          
        
          Removing them means being asked again the next time you convert
          an image from them.
          
        
          The add-on asks this only to make it easier for you, because it
          looks like you want to remove permissions. Clicking “Keep current
          permissions” does not turn access to every site back on.
